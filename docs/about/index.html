ONE COMPANY. EXPERTS IN EVERY TRADE.

Buddy Baths is the one-day shower division of Buddy Built, a family of home-service brands built on one idea: hire the company, not a stranger from a listing. One standard, one warranty, one number to call.

Every Buddy crew works to one standard, carries one warranty, and answers to one phone number. Your designer measures the bathroom with you, brings the wall, glass, and hardware samples to your home, and your written price lands in your inbox the same day.

Our shower and bath systems are made in the USA: non-porous high-tech polymer and PuroStone walls, built to order for your bathroom, with a lifetime warranty on every system. Buddy Baths designs and installs them across Southwest Washington and the Portland metro.

Want the rest of the bathroom done too, tile floors, a new vanity, paint? That's the point of the family: one call brings the right Buddy trade, and everything lands on one schedule and one invoice.
