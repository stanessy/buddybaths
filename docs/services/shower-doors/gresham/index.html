QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

Can you replace just my shower door?

Yes. If the base and walls are sound, a new door or enclosure is a half-day job. We measure the opening and order the glass to fit.

Which glass is easiest to keep clean?

Clear glass shows water spots the most. Rain and frosted glass hide them and still let light in. A quick squeegee after each shower keeps any of them looking new.
