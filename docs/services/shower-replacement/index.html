QUESTIONS WE HEAR EVERY WEEK

How much does a shower replacement cost in Vancouver WA?

Most one-day shower replacements run $9,000-$17,000 installed, depending on size, wall finish, glass style, and whether the plumbing moves. Your written in-home estimate is free and the price does not change unless you change the plan.

Is a one-day shower really done in one day?

For most straight swaps, yes: tear-out in the morning, new base and walls by afternoon, door hung before we leave. Projects that need plumbing or subfloor repair, or a special-order color, can add a day or two. We tell you the timeline before you sign.

What are the walls made of?

Our wall systems are a non-porous high-tech polymer that resists chips, stains, mold, and mildew. There is no grout to scrub and no tile to crack. Every system carries a lifetime warranty.

Can I pick the color and tile pattern?

Yes. Choose from 30 colors and finishes and 17 laser-etched patterns like subway, herringbone, and hexagonal, plus glass and hardware finishes. See the Design Options page for the full list.
