DESIGN YOUR SHOWER

Every Buddy Baths shower is built from four choices: the wall finish, the pattern etched into it, the glass, and the hardware finish. Pick what you like here, and we bring the real samples to your bathroom at the free design visit.

Step 1

PUROSTONE ENGINEERED STONE

The premium wall. Matte, warm to the touch, non-porous, GREENGUARD certified, and backed by a lifetime limited warranty. Five natural stone colors.

Step 1, continued

WALL COLORS & FINISHES

Seamless high-tech polymer panels in marble, travertine, concrete, and solid looks. Non-porous, no grout, cleans with soap and water.

Also available: Brecchia · Sierra Sand · Madeira · Santa Cruz Granite · River Rock · Canyon Rock · Travertine · Lightning · Quartz · Silver White · Gray · Almond · Biscuit · Sandbar · Evo · Metapeake · Napoli Marble · Pompeii Marble · San Michele · Versailles

Step 2

LASER-ETCHED TILE PATTERNS

The look of real tile without a single grout line. Patterns are laser-etched into the wall panel, so the texture is real and the maintenance is not.

Also available: 8x10 · Bayview · Fairfield · Flagstone · Hopscotch · Milano · Panorama

Step 3

GLASS & ENCLOSURES

Sliding or swinging doors in 1/4", 5/16", or 3/8" glass. Four finishes, plus V-groove patterns (Arrow, Hopscotch, Roman Block, Vertical Block) on select models.

Step 4

HARDWARE FINISHES

Door hardware, grab bars, shower heads, and towel bars all come in the same four finishes, so everything matches.

  • Matte Black
  • Brushed Nickel
  • Oil-Rubbed Bronze
  • Chrome
