FAQ

Questions we hear every week

How much does a shower replacement cost in Vancouver WA?

Most one-day shower replacements run $9,000-$17,000 installed, depending on size, wall finish, glass style, and whether the plumbing moves. Your written in-home estimate is free and the price does not change unless you change the plan.

Is a one-day shower really done in one day?

For most straight swaps, yes: tear-out in the morning, new base and walls by afternoon, door hung before we leave. Projects that need plumbing or subfloor repair, or a special-order color, can add a day or two. We tell you the timeline before you sign.

How much does a custom tile shower cost in Vancouver WA?

Most full tile showers run $7,000-$10,500 for labor and waterproofing, depending on size and features like niches, benches, or curbless entries. Finish tile is priced with your selections, and your written in-home estimate is free.

How long does a custom tile shower take?

Most showers run 5-8 working days from demo to grout: tear-out, waterproofing with a flood test, tile, then grout and seal. If you need it faster and like a seamless look, ask about our one-day shower systems.

How much does a full bathroom remodel cost?

Full remodels are priced from a written scope, because tile, vanity, fixtures, and any plumbing or wall changes drive the number. Your free in-home design visit produces that scope and price the same day, and it does not change unless you change the plan.

One-day shower or custom tile: which is right for me?

Pick one-day if you want a clean, modern shower fast, with no grout and a lifetime warranty on the walls. Pick custom if you want a specific tile, a new layout, heated floors, or the whole bathroom redone. Not sure? The free design visit covers both, and we price whichever fits.
